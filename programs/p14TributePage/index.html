Ideas for a brighter future...

Photo of Jacques Fresco at the Venus Project in florida

Jacque Fresco is a futurist, engineer, and philosopher. He created "the Venus Project", a movement to transform a society that is resource-based, efficient, and sustainable. He's made several documentaries on Netflix such as Future by Design, The Venus Project, and Cities in the Sea. He currently resides in Florida with his partner Roxanne Meadows.

Jacques is a centenarian and has many accomplishments. Here is a list of some of them:

  1. 1915 - Born in New York City.
  2. 1942 - Drafted to the US army.
  3. 1969 - Co-authored "Moving Foward".
  4. 2006 - Completed "Future by Design".
  5. 2010 - Started the Venus Project.
