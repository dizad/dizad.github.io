18. Values:

18.1. Quality:
  • Delivering a product that meets the customer's expectations and builds trust and branding as a result.
  • Paying attention to details and using mechanisms for double checking the product before delivery.
18.2. Innovation:
  • Continuously questioning ways for improving an existing system.
  • Keeping an open-mind on new technologies and experimenting with them to find their potential.
18.3. Sustainability:
  • Looking at the forest instead of the tree, and looking at the impact of a solution in terms of the entire supply chain.
18.4. Customer Service:
  • Supporting a product after its release and assuring that the product continues to satisfy the customer.
  • Maintaining credibility, delivering on deadlines, and communicating roadblocks ahead of time.
18.5. Lean:
  • Continuously removing waste from a system i.e. defects, motion, inventory, unutilized talent, waiting, or overproduction.
18.6. Accuracy:
  • Seeking the truth by researching and experimenting instead of assuming. Practicing "Genchi Genbutsu" i.e. "In order to learn, you must go and see".
