PROJECTS:

Dan made 25 movies to date, each ranging from 2 to 30 mins long. He uses this skill to present complex designs, concepts, and systems to co-workers, customers, and stakeholders. This helps communicate engineering designs to an audience that is not always technically-savvy. Some of his videos were shown at corporate summits, local television stations, and training modules.

2D / 3D ANIMATION:

Dan creates 2D animations using frame-by-frame, motion-key-driven, or shape-key-driven animation. The upper banner animation on this website is a good 2D animation example showing all 3 of these techniques into one. Dan also creates 3D animations using CGI (Computer Generated Imagery). The CGI process involves modeling, texturing / UV mapping, rigging / skinning, animating, simulating (dynamics), lighting, and rendering. Dan also has experience designing vehicles, characters, and concept designs related to the entertainment industry. Dan mainly uses Photoshop and a WACOM tablet to digitally paint his artwork.
